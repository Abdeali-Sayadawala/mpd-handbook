Identity check (KYC)

We check who you are once, so your property and your payments stay protected.

What to send

DocumentWhat we ask for
PAN cardThe 10-character PAN, for example ABCDE1234F
AadhaarOnly the last 4 digits. We never ask for, and never keep, the full number.
PassportThe passport number
OCI cardThe OCI card number
Other IDJust the photo or scan

We show your number masked, for example XXXX XXXX 1234, everywhere. The photo or scan is kept in your private vault.

Steps

  1. Open Identity (KYC) and choose Add a document.
  2. Pick the document, enter the number it asks for, and choose a clear photo or scan (all four corners and all the text visible).
  3. Choose Send for review. The status shows Waiting for review. Our team usually checks within one working day.
  4. When it is accepted the status shows Verified. You are done.

If it is rejected

You will see the reason, for example "the photo is blurred". Remove that document and send a new one.

Good to know

  • One verified identity document (PAN, Aadhaar, passport or OCI) is enough to be marked Verified.
  • You can send more than one kind. If an extra one is rejected later, your Verified status stays.
  • A verified document cannot be removed by you. If something is wrong, contact us.
  • Only our verification team can see the scan, and each time they open it is recorded.
