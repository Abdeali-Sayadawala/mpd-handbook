Common questions

Do I need a project or a code for my own house?

No. A standalone house, flat or plot is added under Properties. Projects are only for builders and societies.

The app says it is not serving my area. What now?

Save your property anyway. We open areas as soon as we have a trained local partner there, and we will let you know.

Do you store my Aadhaar number?

No. We ask only for the last 4 digits, and show it as XXXX XXXX 1234. The photo of your document stays in your private vault and only our verification team can open it.

Who can see my documents?

Only you. Our verification team opens identity documents and owner-registration proof to check them, and every time is recorded. A share link you create works only for the time and number of openings you choose.

I lost the share link I created.

For safety it is shown only once. Cancel the old one and create a new link.

My invitation link does not work.

Invitation links work once and expire (14 days for owner registrations, 7 days for staff). Ask whoever invited you to send it again.

The builder registered the wrong person as owner.

The builder can withdraw a registration until it is accepted. After an owner accepts, only our team can change it, with a reason, and the history is kept.

I filled in the form. What happens now?

Our team contacts you. Create an account with the same email address and open Getting started to follow your request. See the steps.

How is the price decided?

Each service is charged in one way: a fixed price, per square foot, per hour or similar. The amount depends on the pincode, so the same service can cost differently in different areas. A small job has a minimum charge. You see the exact total before you buy, and it never changes after you agree.

What happens to a property's history when it is sold?

The history belongs to the property. The new owner sees what was arranged before, but not the earlier owner's name or what they paid.

Why does the same plan cost different amounts for different properties?

Services in a plan that are charged by size, such as per square foot, depend on your property. A plan made only of fixed-price services costs the same everywhere. The price is shown to you, with every step, before you buy.

Can I buy a single service without a plan?

Yes. Plans are optional. Any service can be bought on its own, at any time.

How do I pay?

After you place an order it waits for payment. You pay our team by bank transfer, cheque, UPI or cash (builders and societies always pay this way). We record your payment with the proof and a second person checks and approves it, so the order shows Paid. Online payment coming soon

Why does my payment need two people?

So no single person can mark an order paid by mistake or on their own. One records the money, a different one checks it against the bank.
