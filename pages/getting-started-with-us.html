Your request and your offer

New to My Property Doctors? Tell us what you need. We look after you step by step, and you always see where you are.

The steps

  1. Tell us what you need. Fill in the "Talk to us" form on our website: your name, email, where you live, the pincode of your property and what you need. You do not need an account yet.
  2. We contact you. The team that looks after your property's area, together with our operations team, follows up.
  3. Create your account. Sign up with the same email address you used in the form. When you open Getting started in your account, your request is there.
  4. Add your property and choose it for your request, so we can price things for the right place and size.
  5. A site visit, if it helps. For some properties we visit first. This is a paid visit, ordered and paid like any other order. If it is not needed, we skip it and tell you why.
  6. You get an offer. It lists the services (or a plan), the price for your property, any discount and why, and how long the offer stays open (usually 14 days).
  7. Accept or decline. Accepting creates your order at the price in the offer. If you decline, tell us what to change and we send a new one.
  8. Pay. We record your payment with the proof, a second person checks it, and you are active.

Good to know

  • The price in an offer does not change while it is open, even if our prices change.
  • If an offer expires, ask us for a new one.
  • Our internal notes about your request are never shown to you or to anyone outside our team.
  • If you asked twice with the same email, we keep it as one request.
  • You can see where you are at any time under Getting started in your account.
