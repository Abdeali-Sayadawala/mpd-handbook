Everyone sees and manages only their own branch. Nobody can invite someone above their own level.

Areas

Areas are managed by pincode. A group of pincodes with a name is called a territory (for example "Alkapuri side": 390007, 390015). A franchise's pincodes must be inside its master franchise's, and a provider's inside its franchise's, so nobody can claim an area their parent does not hold.

When can customers use an area? When the pincode is switched on and at least one active provider with active staff covers it. Otherwise the customer sees "we are not serving this area yet".

Setting up the network Live

  1. Organisations: add a master franchise, then franchises and service providers under it. Give each a territory.
  2. Staff: invite the person by email. They open the link, choose a password and are signed in. Invitations work once and expire in 7 days.
  3. Masters, Pincodes: switch a pincode on when you are ready to open it.

Prices Live

  1. Pricing, Rate types: how a service is charged: a lump sum, per sq ft, per hour, or one you add (per day, per visit…).
  2. Pricing, Price lists: create a draft (or copy the current list). Prices are changed in a draft, then published from a date. The previous list ends the day before. A published list cannot be edited, so what customers agreed to never changes.
  3. Pricing, Service prices: for each service choose how it is charged, then set a default price, an optional minimum charge and a price for any pincode that differs. You can paste many lines like 390002, 15.50 at once. A line shows how many open pincodes have a price. A service with no price in an area is not offered there.

Reviewing customers Live

  • KYC review: open the scan, check it against the number shown, then verify or reject with a reason the customer will read. Each time you open a scan it is recorded under your name.
  • Customers: find a customer and see their properties. Operations can set up a customer as a project owner (a builder or society).
  • Owner registrations: a project owner says who owns a unit and attaches proof. Open the proof, then Approve (the owner is emailed an invitation) or Reject with a reason. You can also send an invitation again, or Revoke an owner with a reason. Changing an existing owner is always reviewed.

Orders Live

  • Orders: customers' purchases, waiting for payment, paid or cancelled. Find an order by its reference (for example O-7K3M9QX2). You can cancel an unpaid order with a reason, whoever bought it. The reason is recorded.
  • The property owner and the project owner can each buy for a property. Only the one who bought can change it; you can override with a reason. Taking payment (offline, approved by a second person) is coming soon.

Website and services Live

  • Service catalogue: edit names, descriptions and order, and switch a service or category on or off. A switched-off service disappears from the website and the app within seconds, and cannot be bought. Existing jobs are not affected.
  • Website content: the homepage text, the team, FAQs and testimonials.

How requests will work Coming soon

  1. A customer buys a service. It becomes a request that every eligible provider can see: providers who work in that pincode and have chosen that service.
  2. The franchise, master franchise and our operations team can see it too, and can ask a provider to take it.
  3. The first provider to accept gets the job.
  4. The provider assigns one of their workers, who must have chosen that service, and the worker does the job.

Providers will choose their services, and workers the ones they can do, in their own screens. Customer name, phone and address stay hidden until a provider accepts.
