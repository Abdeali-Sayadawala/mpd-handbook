For our team and partners

How the team and partners use the staff console at /admin. Staff sign in on their own page, separate from customers.

Everyone sees and manages only their own branch. Nobody can invite someone above their own level.

Areas

Areas are managed by pincode. A group of pincodes with a name is called a territory (for example "Alkapuri side": 390007, 390015). A franchise's pincodes must be inside its master franchise's, and a provider's inside its franchise's, so nobody can claim an area their parent does not hold.

When can customers use an area? When the pincode is switched on and at least one active provider with active staff covers it. Otherwise the customer sees "we are not serving this area yet".

Setting up the network Live

  1. Organisations: add a master franchise, then franchises and service providers under it. Give each a territory.
  2. Staff: invite the person by email. They open the link, choose a password and are signed in. Invitations work once and expire in 7 days.
  3. Masters, Pincodes: switch a pincode on when you are ready to open it.

Prices Live

  1. Pricing, Rate types: how a service is charged: a lump sum, per sq ft, per hour, or one you add (per day, per visit…).
  2. Pricing, Price lists: create a draft (or copy the current list). Prices are changed in a draft, then published from a date. The previous list ends the day before. A published list cannot be edited, so what customers agreed to never changes.
  3. Pricing, Service prices: for each service choose how it is charged, then set a default price, an optional minimum charge and a price for any pincode that differs. You can paste many lines like 390002, 15.50 at once. A line shows how many open pincodes have a price. A service with no price in an area is not offered there.

Reviewing customers Live

  • KYC review: open the scan, check it against the number shown, then verify or reject with a reason the customer will read. Each time you open a scan it is recorded under your name.
  • Customers: find a customer and see their properties. Operations can set up a customer as a project owner (a builder or society).
  • Owner registrations: a project owner says who owns a unit and attaches proof. Open the proof, then Approve (the owner is emailed an invitation) or Reject with a reason. You can also send an invitation again, or Revoke an owner with a reason. Changing an existing owner is always reviewed.

Doing the work Live

  1. The provider (or operations) sets the visit time; the customer sees it.
  2. The worker presses I am on the way. If they cannot go they press I cannot attend and give a reason: the job returns to the provider, who picks someone else.
  3. At the property the customer opens their request and shows a start code. The worker types it and presses Start the work.
  4. When finished, the customer shows a finish code. The worker types it, writes what was done and presses Finish the work. For a site visit the worker also fills in any details that were wrong (address, pincode, area); this verifies the property.
  5. The provider or operations check the work, or send it back with a reason (the worker finishes again with a new code). A checked job can then be rated by the customer and closed.
  6. If a customer cannot give a code (for example abroad), operations can start or finish without a code, with a written reason. This is recorded.
  7. Once work has started a job cannot be cancelled.

Checklists and photos Live

Operations write a checklist for each service in Service catalogue (the list icon on the service): the steps a worker must do, whether each is a tick, a note, a number or a choice, which are required, which need a photo, and whether a before and after photo is required. Saving makes a new version; visits already started keep the version they began with.

The worker sees the checklist on the job once the work has started. They answer each step and add photos (before, after, or for a step); the phone records the time and, if allowed, the place. They cannot finish until everything required is done; the screen says what is missing. Providers, franchises and operations can read the checklist, the answers and the photos on the job. The customer sees it all once the work is done.

Legal requests Live

Operations set up the form and the document checklist of each legal service under Legal service forms, and see what customers send under Legal requests: the customer's contact, every answer and each document (a link that works for five minutes and is recorded under your name). A customer's unfinished draft is private and is never shown. Open a request to send an offer (a line for each part: your fee, government fees, stamp duty; an optional discount with a reason; the days it stays open), ask for more, or say we cannot take it with a reason the customer reads. When the customer accepts, an order is made; record its payment as usual and a second person approves it. Once the payment is approved a legal case opens (see below).

Legal cases Live

A legal case is a paid legal service waiting for a legal partner. A legal partner is a normal service provider that offers a legal service (tick it under the organisation's Services) and holds the area. Every such provider sees the case under Legal cases → My pool with the area and the order total, but not the customer. The first to accept wins. After accepting you see the customer, their answers and documents (links work for five minutes and are recorded) and can message them; press Work has begun when you start, or Give it back before that. The franchise and operations can nudge providers or give the case to a provider; a request that was not about a property has no area and operations give it out by hand. If nobody takes a case within a day operations are alerted. Operations can cancel a case. Once work has begun, tick each step of the service as you finish it, deliver the finished documents (they go into the customer's own vault) and press Complete the case when every step is ticked and one document is delivered. The customer then rates the work, and you are told the rating.

Earnings and payouts Live

When a job is closed, the organisations on it earn a share of what the customer paid for it (including any extra work the customer approved and paid): the service provider that did the job, and the franchise and master franchise of its area. Operations set the share of each level (one default for each level, and a different share for a service category if needed); the three can never add up to more than the whole price, and MPD keeps the rest. A cancelled job earns nothing, and changing a share never changes what was already earned. Open Earnings to see what is waiting to be paid, what is in a payout and what has been paid, and what each job earned. Payouts are sent outside the app for now: one admin records the payout with the bank reference and the date, and a different admin approves it. You get a message when it is approved. Providers see their own earnings; franchises and master franchises see theirs; workers do not see earnings (the provider pays its workers).

Reports Live

Everyone on the team has Reports in the menu, showing only what their role may see. Operations see everything: money received and still unpaid, jobs requested, completed and cancelled, how long providers take to accept, how often the acceptance limit is missed, how long work takes, customer ratings, and a table for each provider and each pincode. A franchise or master franchise sees the same for its own area. A provider's admin sees its organisation's jobs and a table for each worker (no money; earnings come later). A worker sees their own jobs and rating. Choose the period (7, 30 or 90 days, this month, or your own dates). Operations, franchises and provider admins can download the jobs as a spreadsheet.

Messages on a job Live

Each job has a chat once a provider has taken it. The customer, the assigned worker, the provider's admins and operations can write; a franchise or master franchise in the area can read. Messages are text only and are never edited or deleted. The job list and the Jobs board show how many messages are new, and you are told in the bell. The chat closes for writing when the job is closed or cancelled.

Notifications Live

Everyone who signs in has a bell at the top with the number of unread messages. A worker is told when a job is given to them, when work is sent back, and when the customer approves or pays for extra work. A provider's admin is told when a worker cannot attend, when work is ready to check, and when large extra work needs checking. Customers are told how their request is going. A provider's admins are also told when a new job is waiting in their pool and when the upline nudges. Workers get a reminder the day before a visit and two hours before. If nobody accepts a job in time, operations and the area's franchise are alerted. Pressing a message opens the right page. Email copies can be switched off on the Notifications page.

The worker's phone screen Live

A field worker opens My day on their phone. It lists their jobs, today's first, with the address, a Directions button, a Call button and the next step in plain words. The big buttons are made for thumbs. The worker can add the site to the phone's home screen (browser menu, Add to Home screen) so it opens like an app. There is no separate app to download.

Extra work Live

If the worker finds more work than was booked, they press More work is needed on the job. They list the items and prices, explain why, and add photos. The job then waits. If the amount is large (over half the price of the visit) the provider or operations check it first. The customer approves, which makes an order to pay (we record the payment and a second person approves it), or declines. The worker carries on only after the payment is approved. If the customer declines, the worker finishes what was agreed. Providers and operations can cancel a request with a reason.

Jobs and the pool Live

  1. A paid service (or a visit from a plan) becomes a job in the pool. Every provider that covers the pincode and offers the service sees it under Jobs → My pool, with the area, property type and size but not the customer's name, phone or full address.
  2. A provider presses Accept. The first one wins; the others are told it is taken. Turn down keeps it in the pool for others and hides it from you. Give back returns an accepted job to the pool (with a reason).
  3. After accepting, the provider sees the customer's details and chooses a worker: only its own active workers who can do that service. The worker can be changed until the work starts. A worker sees only their own jobs.
  4. A franchise or master franchise sees the jobs in its area. It can ask providers to take it (at most every 15 minutes) or give it to an eligible provider with a reason. Operations see everything and can cancel a job that has not started, with a reason.
  5. A job still waiting after two hours (30 minutes if urgent) is flagged Overdue. Automatic alerts come later.

Areas and services Live

A franchise's pincodes can never overlap another franchise's, and each city has one master franchise; the system refuses a clash and tells you which franchise holds the pincode. Service providers can overlap. A provider chooses the services it offers in Organisations → Services; each worker then chooses which of those they can do under People → Services. Anyone above a provider can switch one of its services off with a reason (the provider cannot do this to itself); a switched-off service cannot be given to workers until it is switched back on.

Providers under several franchises Live

A provider is registered under the franchise it was created below. The provider's admin chooses the pincodes it serves (map icon on its row in Organisations), always from the pincodes of a franchise it is registered with. To serve pincodes run by another franchise, open Organisations → Franchises on the provider and ask that franchise. The franchise sees the request under Providers and accepts or rejects it. Only after it accepts can the provider hold that franchise's pincodes. If a registration ends, the pincodes only that franchise covered are taken off the provider.

Site visits Live

Site visits lists paid visits waiting for someone to go. After the visit, press Visit done: write what you found and fill in only the details that were wrong on file (address, pincode, area). This verifies the property and lets the owner buy services and plans. A visit can be completed once. When an owner orders for several properties as a batch, record one payment for the batch from Orders ("Record batch payment"); approving it pays every order in it.

Onboarding Live

  1. Onboarding lists everyone who asked about our services: from the website form, a phone call or a referral. A request is automatically handed to the franchise whose area holds the property's pincode (or the master franchise). Cases that need attention (a new request older than a day, or nothing done for three days) are flagged.
  2. Franchises and master franchises see the cases of their own area and can read them, add internal notes and mark that they contacted the person. Everything else on a case is done by operations.
  3. Operations link the customer's account (it also links by itself when they sign up with the same email), choose the property, then either charge for a site visit (an order for a site-visit service; the case moves on when it is paid, and you mark it done with a note) or skip it with a reason.
  4. Make an offer: add services (with a quantity where they are charged per hour, day or visit) or one plan. The price is worked out for the customer's property. You can add a discount, but must say why. Choose how many days it stays open. Sending a new offer withdraws the old one.
  5. When the customer accepts, an order is created at the quoted price. Record and approve its payment as usual (see Payments). When it is paid, the customer is active.
  6. Close a case with a reason if it goes nowhere; you can reopen it later.

Plans Live

  1. Pricing, Plans, New plan: name, description, how many months it lasts. Search the catalogue and add the services. For each service say how many times it is done during the plan (for example 4 pest checks), how many each time if it is charged per hour, day or visit, an optional discount % on that service, and a note like "every 3 months". You can also give a discount % on the whole plan.
  2. You never type a plan price. For each property it is worked out from the services' own prices: one visit's price (a fixed price, or the property size times the rate, or the quantity times the rate, with the minimum charge) times how many times, less the service discount, then less the plan discount. A plan of only fixed-price services costs the same everywhere.
  3. Try a price shows what a customer would pay for a pincode and size, with every step.
  4. A plan can only be sold where every service in it has a price. If a service is switched off, the plan is blocked until it is back or removed.
  5. Switch a plan off to stop selling it. Plans already bought carry on. Editing a plan changes only new purchases.

Orders Live

  • Orders: customers' purchases, waiting for payment, paid or cancelled. Find an order by its reference (for example O-7K3M9QX2). You can cancel an unpaid order with a reason, whoever bought it. The reason is recorded.
  • The property owner and the project owner can each buy for a property. Only the one who bought can change it; you can override with a reason.

Payments Live

  1. Orders, Record payment (the maker): choose how it was paid, enter the reference (UTR, cheque number or UPI reference; not needed for cash), the day the money arrived, and attach the proof (bank slip, cheque image or screenshot). The amount must equal the order total. The order is not paid yet.
  2. Payments, Waiting for approval (the checker): a different person opens the proof, checks it against the bank, and chooses Approve or Reject with a reason. You cannot approve or reject a payment you recorded yourself.
  3. Approving marks the order paid and starts a plan. Rejecting leaves it unpaid; the payment can be recorded again. If you recorded something by mistake, Withdraw it.
  4. The same bank reference or the same slip cannot be used twice. While a payment is waiting, the order cannot be cancelled.
  5. Every step is recorded with who did it. The proof is kept in the customer's vault, and each time it is opened is logged.

Website and services Live

  • Service catalogue: edit names, descriptions and order, and switch a service or category on or off. A switched-off service disappears from the website and the app within seconds, and cannot be bought. Existing jobs are not affected.
  • Website content: the homepage text, the team, FAQs and testimonials.

How requests will work Coming soon

  1. A customer buys a service. It becomes a request that every eligible provider can see: providers who work in that pincode and have chosen that service.
  2. The franchise, master franchise and our operations team can see it too, and can ask a provider to take it.
  3. The first provider to accept gets the job.
  4. The provider assigns one of their workers, who must have chosen that service, and the worker does the job.

Providers will choose their services, and workers the ones they can do, in their own screens. Customer name, phone and address stay hidden until a provider accepts.
