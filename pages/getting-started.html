Getting started

Create your account, add your first property and check whether we serve the area. It takes about five minutes.

1. Create your account

  1. Open the website and choose Sign up. Enter your name, email and a password of at least 10 characters.
  2. We email you a 6-digit code. Enter it on the next page. The code works for 10 minutes and allows 5 attempts.
  3. You are signed in and see your dashboard.

Forgot your password? Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page. We email you a link to choose a new one.

2. Add a property

  1. Open Properties and choose Add a property.
  2. Give it a name you will recognise, for example "Family flat, Vadodara".
  3. Choose the type (for example Residential, then Flat - 2 BHK). A few questions about that type appear. Answer what you know.
  4. Enter the address and the 6-digit pincode. As soon as you finish typing the pincode we show the place and tell you whether our services are available there.
  5. Add the built-up area and its unit (square feet, square metres and so on). Some services are priced by size, so this matters. You can add it later.
  6. Save. You can edit the property, or archive it when you no longer need it. Nothing is ever deleted by accident.
"We are not serving this area yet." You can still save the property. We will let you know when we start there.

3. Next steps

  • Put your papers in the document vault.
  • Complete your identity check.
  • See prices and buy services for your property.

Your time zone

Dates and times in the app show in your own time zone, and in Indian time where it matters. You can change your time zone and country under Profile.
