Document vault

One private place for every paper about your property. Only you can open it, and every opening is recorded.

Folders

Choose My documents for papers about you, or pick one of your properties.

FolderFor
OwnershipSale deed, title papers, mutation records (per property)
Tax & utilitiesProperty tax receipts, electricity and water bills (per property)
Legal & agreementsPower of attorney, rent or lease agreements (per property)
Inspection reportsReports from our visits (per property)
Job evidencePhotos and proof from work done (per property)
KYC documentsYour identity documents (about you)
RegistrationsProof for owners you register, if you are a builder or society
GeneralAnything else (about you)

You can also add your own folders, for example "Insurance".

Add a document

  1. Open Documents, pick a folder and choose Upload. On a phone you can also choose Take a photo.
  2. Choose PDF, JPG, PNG, HEIC or Word (DOCX) files, up to 20 MB each. We check the real type of the file, so a file that is renamed to look like a PDF is refused.
  3. Give it a title. Optionally choose its type, and the date it ends (a rental agreement or insurance policy). Documents ending within 30 days are marked so you notice.
If you upload exactly the same file twice into one folder, we tell you and keep one copy.

Newer versions

When a document is replaced, for example a renewed insurance policy, open it and choose Upload a new version. Older versions are kept and you can still open them.

Opening and downloading

Choose View or Download. The link we create works for only 5 minutes, so a copied link soon stops working.

Share with a lawyer or bank

  1. Open the document and choose the Sharing tab.
  2. Choose how long the link works (1 hour to 7 days) and how many times it can be opened (1 to 20), then Create link.
  3. Copy the link and send it. It is shown only once. The person opens it without needing an account.
  4. You can cancel a link at any time. It also stops working when the time or the number of openings runs out.

Who has looked at my document?

The Activity tab lists every upload, view, download and share, with the time and who did it. When someone opens a document through a share link it shows as "someone with a share link".

Removing a document

Choose Remove on the Details tab. It disappears from your vault and its share links stop working. Documents that are being used for your identity check or for an owner registration cannot be removed until that is finished.

Our team and your documents

Only you can see your documents. Our verification team opens your identity documents and the proof you attach to an owner registration, only to check them, and every time is recorded under their name.
