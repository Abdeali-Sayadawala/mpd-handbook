For builders and societies

If you manage a project with many flats or units, you can add them all and register each owner. The owner is invited, accepts, and the property becomes theirs in the app.

Two kinds of customer

Property ownerProject owner
WhoA person or family who owns propertyA builder, society or association that manages many units
How they joinSign up themselves, or are registered by a project ownerSet up by our team, after identity verification

An individual owner does not need a project. A standalone bungalow or flat is added under Properties and needs no project and no code.

Getting set up

Contact us. We verify your identity and set your account up as a project owner. A Projects menu then appears for you.

Create a project and add units

  1. Open Projects and choose New project. Enter the name, type (builder project, society or other), address and pincode. The project gets a short code.
  2. Open the project and choose Add a unit. Use the unit number as the name, for example A-101. A unit is filled in like any property (type, address, size).

Register the owner of a unit

  1. Next to a unit choose Register owner. Enter the owner's full name, email, optional phone number, and whether they are the allottee, buyer or owner.
  2. Add proof that the property belongs to them: an allotment letter, agreement or sale deed. At least one document is required. It is stored privately in your Registrations vault folder.
  3. Choose Send for review. Our team checks the proof. If it is correct they approve it, and the owner is emailed an invitation. If not, they reject it with a reason you can read, and you can register again.
  4. The owner opens the invitation (valid for 14 days, used once). Someone new chooses a password and is signed in. Someone who already has an account signs in with the invited email and accepts.
  5. The unit now shows Owner registered. The owner sees the property in their own account and receives a copy of the proof in the property's Ownership folder.
Changing an owner. If a unit already has a registered owner, Change owner starts a new registration. It is always reviewed by our team, and the old owner is replaced only when the new owner accepts. The history is kept. You cannot remove an active owner yourself; only our team can, with a reason.

Can I change the owner's property?

Once an owner is registered they control the property details, and you can still see the unit. Before an owner is registered you can edit the unit yourself.

Buying services for units Live

You can buy services for any unit in your project. The owner of a unit can also buy for their own unit. Whoever bought a service is the only one who can change it. The other side can see that it exists, but not its price. See buying services. You pay offline (bank transfer or cheque). Our team records your payment with the proof and a second person approves it, then the order shows Paid.
