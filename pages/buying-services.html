Buying services

Choose what your property needs, see the exact price, and place the order. The price never changes after you order.

How prices work

  • Every service is charged in one way: a fixed price, per square foot, per hour, per visit and so on.
  • The amount depends on your pincode, so the same service can cost differently in different areas.
  • Services charged by size use the built-up area of your property. Add it under Properties if the price shows "add the area".
  • Small jobs have a minimum charge.
  • If we do not serve your area yet, or a service has no price there, it is simply not shown.

Place an order

  1. Open your property and choose Buy services. You can search the list.
  2. Choose Add to order on each service you want. For services charged per hour, visit or similar, enter how many.
  3. The box on the right shows each price and the total, worked out by us. Nothing here can be changed by editing the page.
  4. If someone has already arranged the same service for this property (you, or the project owner or the owner), we tell you who, so you do not pay twice. Tick the box only if you still want it.
  5. Choose Place order. Your order shows as Waiting for payment under Orders. Our team arranges payment with you. Online payment coming soon

Who can change an order?

Only the person who placed it. This applies in both directions:

Our team can cancel an unpaid order if asked, and always records why. If ownership of a property changes, the earlier purchases stay with the person who bought them.

Plans

A plan bundles visits of several services for a set period at one price, for example a year of care for your property. Our team makes the plans, so what is on offer can change.

  1. Open your property and choose Plans.
  2. Each plan shows its price for your area, how long it lasts, and the services and number of visits included.
  3. Choose Choose this plan, check the price and buy. If the same plan already runs on the property, we tell you who has it.
  4. The plan starts when payment is approved and lasts the number of months shown. Under Plans on this property you see how many visits are left of each service.

You can still buy single services at any time, with or without a plan.

History of a property

Open a property and choose History. It lists everything arranged for the property, newest first: orders, plans and changes of owner. It stays with the property. If you buy a property, you see what was arranged before you, but not the earlier owner's name or what they paid. Visits and reports will appear here too coming soon.

Cancel an order

Open Orders and choose Cancel order on one that is still waiting for payment. A paid order cannot be cancelled here; contact us.
