Buying services

Choose what your property needs, see the exact price, and place the order. The price never changes after you order.

First, a site visit Live

Every property starts with a site visit. A person goes to your property, checks it and corrects the details we hold (address, size, type). You book and pay for it like any other service. Until it is done you cannot choose other services or plans for that property. Afterwards, those checked details are locked so prices stay fair; contact us if something really changes.

How prices work

  • Every service is charged in one way: a fixed price, per square foot, per hour, per visit and so on.
  • The amount depends on your pincode, so the same service can cost differently in different areas.
  • Services charged by size use the built-up area of your property. Add it under Properties if the price shows "add the area".
  • Small jobs have a minimum charge.
  • If we do not serve your area yet, or a service has no price there, it is simply not shown.

Place an order

  1. Open your property and choose Buy services. You can search the list.
  2. Choose Add to order on each service you want. For services charged per hour, visit or similar, enter how many.
  3. The box on the right shows each price and the total, worked out by us. Nothing here can be changed by editing the page.
  4. If someone has already arranged the same service for this property (you, or the project owner or the owner), we tell you who, so you do not pay twice. Tick the box only if you still want it.
  5. Choose Place order. Your order shows as Waiting for payment under Orders.
  6. Pay our team by bank transfer, cheque, UPI or cash, as agreed. We record your payment with the proof, a second person checks it, and then your order shows Paid. A plan starts on that day. Online payment coming soon

Who can change an order?

Only the person who placed it. This applies in both directions:

Our team can cancel an unpaid order if asked, and always records why. If ownership of a property changes, the earlier purchases stay with the person who bought them.

Plans

A plan bundles visits of several services for a set period at one price, for example a year of care for your property. Our team makes the plans, so what is on offer can change.

  1. Open your property and choose Plans.
  2. Each plan shows how long it lasts and which services it includes, and how often each is done. Its price is worked out for your property: a plan of fixed-price services costs the same everywhere, while services charged by size (per square foot) follow the size of your property. Add your built-up area under Properties if the plan asks for it.
  3. Choose Choose this plan to see how the price is made up: each service, how many times, any discount on it, and any discount on the whole plan. Check it and buy. If the same plan already runs on the property, we tell you who has it.
  4. The plan starts when payment is approved and lasts the number of months shown. Under Plans on this property you see how many visits are left of each service.

You can still buy single services at any time, with or without a plan.

History of a property

Open a property and choose History. It lists everything arranged for the property, newest first: orders, plans and changes of owner. It stays with the property. If you buy a property, you see what was arranged before you, but not the earlier owner's name or what they paid. Visits and reports will appear here too coming soon.

Cancel an order

Open Orders and choose Cancel order on one that is still waiting for payment. If we have already received your payment details and are checking them, the order cannot be cancelled until that check is finished. A paid order cannot be cancelled here; contact us.

How payment is checked

Every payment is handled by two people: one records what was received with the bank slip or cheque image, and a different person checks it against the bank and approves it. Only then is your order marked paid. On your order you see being checked, payment received, or, if something does not match, the reason and what to do. Your slip is kept in your private vault under Payment receipts.

Your service requests Live

Once you have paid for a service it appears under Service requests. We offer it to every provider who works in your area and offers that service; the first to accept gets it, and then assigns one of their workers. You see where it stands. Your name, phone number and full address are not shown to providers until one has accepted. If your plan includes visits, press Request a visit (or Urgent) to use one; if you cancel before the work starts the visit goes back into your plan. Only the person who bought the plan can request its visits.

The visit itself Live

We tell you the planned visit time under Service requests. When the worker is with you, press Show my start code and read the 6 digits to them. The code works for 10 minutes and only for that visit; never share it with anyone else, and never before the worker is there. When the work is done, press Show my finish code in the same way. After we have checked the work you can rate it from 1 to 5.

Once the work is done, open What was done, and photos on your request to see the checklist the worker followed and the before and after photos. The photos are also kept in your vault under the property's Job evidence folder, where only you and our team can open them.

Notifications Live

The bell at the top of every page shows how many new messages you have. We tell you when a provider takes your request, when the visit time is set, when the worker is on the way, when the work is done, when extra work needs your decision, and when we receive your payment (or cannot accept it, with the reason). We also remind you the day before a visit, when an order is still unpaid, before a plan ends (30, 7 and 1 days), and before a document in your vault expires (give a document an end date to get this). Times are shown on your own clock. Press a message to go to the right page. On the Notifications page you can also choose to get a copy by email; the messages in the app are always on.

Legal services: the first step Live

Open Legal services in the menu to ask for a legal or professional service done by our legal partners, such as a sale deed, a title check, a registration, a will or a licence. Choose the service and press Start. Answer the questions (each answer is saved as you go) and add the documents we ask for. Your documents are kept in your vault, in the folder Legal cases. The page lists anything still missing. When everything is there press Send to our team; after that you cannot change the answers, but you can withdraw it. Until you send it, nobody else can see it.

Our team reviews your request. If something is missing they ask you in the bell and on the request page; add it and send the request again. Then they send you a price: each part is on its own line (our fee, government fees, stamp duty), with any discount and the total, and the date until which it stays open. Press Accept and pay or Decline. If you accept, an order appears under Orders; pay it like any other order (we record your payment and a second person approves it). Once the payment is approved your request becomes a case and we find a legal partner in your area. Your request page shows who has taken it, and a message box lets you talk to them. Coming soon: the progress of the work step by step, and the finished documents delivered to your vault.

Messages on a job Live

Once a provider has taken your request, open Messages on it to write to the worker, the provider and our team. You see people by their first name and role, and you are told in the bell when someone replies. Please do not share your start or finish code in a message: show it only when the worker is with you. The chat closes when the job is finished; you can still read it.

Extra work Live

If the worker finds more work than you booked, they send you a request with the reason, photos and a price for each item. Open it under Service requests. Press Approve and pay or Decline. If you approve, an order appears under Orders for you to pay. The worker does not continue until we have approved your payment. If you decline, they finish only what you booked.
