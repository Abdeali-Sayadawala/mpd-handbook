Who does what

Behind the app is a network of local partners. Each one sees only their own part.

Owner of My Property Doctors (super admin) └─ Master franchise runs one city └─ Franchise runs an area in that city └─ Service provider a contractor with a team └─ Service worker does the work at your property
WhoWhat they do
You (customer)Add properties, keep documents, verify identity, buy services, read reports.
Super admin and our operations teamLook after the whole system: services, prices, identity checks, partners.
Master franchiseManages a whole city. Sees everything their franchises, providers and workers do. Invites franchises and service providers.
FranchiseManages an area inside the city, and the providers working there. Invites service providers.
Service providerA contractor. Chooses which services they offer and which areas they work in, manages their workers, accepts requests and assigns a worker. Invites workers.
Service workerDoes the job at your property. Chooses which of the provider's services they can do.

How a request reaches a worker Live

  1. You buy a service for your property.
  2. Every service provider who works in your area and offers that service can see the request. So can the franchise and master franchise for the area, and our operations team.
  3. The first provider to accept gets the job. The franchise or master franchise can also ask a provider to take it.
  4. The provider assigns one of their workers, who goes to your property and does the work.

Your name, phone number and full address stay private until a provider accepts your request.
